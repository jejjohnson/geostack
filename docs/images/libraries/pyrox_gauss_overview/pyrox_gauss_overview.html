pyrox-gauss — one Gaussian, any structure
A Gaussian's covariance or precision is often a structured operator: a Kronecker product over a grid, a sparse precision, a Markov chain in time. pyrox-gauss wraps each as a NumPyro distribution, and gaussx computes its log density, its samples and its KL with that structure, never forming the dense matrix.
supplywhat you write
a mean \boldsymbol{\mu}
a vector, or the prior on the initial state \mathbf{x}_0 of a series
a covariance or a precision
\boldsymbol{\Sigma} or \boldsymbol{\Lambda} as a lineax operator: gx.Kronecker(K_x, K_y), low rank plus diagonal, tridiagonal, dense
or a state-space model
\mathbf{F}, \ \mathbf{H}, \ \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_\eta, \ \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_\varepsilon, and a mask of the observed entries
or a gaussx GMRF field
GaussianMRF, IntrinsicGMRF, ConstrainedGMRF or BYM2GMRF
one densityits cost set by the structure
{\log \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x} \mid \boldsymbol{\mu}, \boldsymbol{\Sigma}) = -\tfrac{1}{2} \left( D_x \log 2\pi + \log \det \boldsymbol{\Sigma} + (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu})^\top \boldsymbol{\Sigma}^{-1} (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu}) \right)}
a Kronecker covariance factorises its two small factors
{\log \det (\mathbf{K}_x \otimes \mathbf{K}_y) = N_y \log \det \mathbf{K}_x + N_x \log \det \mathbf{K}_y}
a precision needs a product and no solve
{(\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu})^\top \boldsymbol{\Lambda} (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu}) - \log \det \boldsymbol{\Lambda}}
a series sums one-step prediction errors, by a Kalman filter
{\log p(\mathbf{y}_{1:T}) = \textstyle\sum_t \log \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{y}_t \mid \mathbf{H} \mathbf{m}_{t \mid t-1}, \mathbf{S}_t)}

Multivariate normals
pyrox_gauss — MultivariateNormal · MultivariateNormalPrecision · AbstractMultivariateNormal, each with a gaussx solver strategy
State-space densities
pyrox_gauss — LGSSM · MaskedLGSSM · LGSSMFactory
Gauss-Markov chains
pyrox_gauss — MarkovGaussian, a density over a state path, in moment or precision form
GMRF fields
pyrox_gauss — GMRFDistribution, any gaussx field as a NumPyro site
KL in closed form is registered with NumPyro's kl_divergence, between these normals and NumPyro's own. pyrox-gauss imports nothing from pyrox.
