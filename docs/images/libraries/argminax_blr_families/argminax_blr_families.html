argminax.blr — one rule, three precisions
A family is a choice of structure for the precision Λθ: each trades storage and the cost of a step against how much of the curvature it can hold.

parameters
any pytree, leaf by leaf

ivon
the diagonal family for deep networks (Shen et al., 2024): momentum on the gradient, a running curvature c̄, a clipped step; Λθ = diag(c̄ + δ)
newton
the full-rank rule with α = 1 and the exact Hessian as the curvature: a damped Newton step inside an optax loop, solved by LU since the Hessian may be indefinite
preconditioned_sgd
the mean form with a precision P the caller fixes; it carries no Gaussian. scale_by_preconditioner is the same step without the step size
blr_posterior
Every learned family reads out as a Laplace whose precision keeps its structure: diagonal, diagonal plus low rank, or dense. ivon needs its parameters and weight decay passed in.
gaussx · lineax
The diagonal step, the low-rank operator and its Woodbury solve, and the dense Cholesky solves.
