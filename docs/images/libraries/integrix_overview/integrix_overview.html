integrix — one integral, three operations
Every integral in the stack is one integral against a measure. A rule sums it, a scan keeps its running value, and a march steps it when the integrand depends on the solution. Adaptivity, adjoints and expectations are shared by all three, and everything is pure JAX, so jit, vmap and grad go through.
one integralan integrand f against a measure \mu on a domain \Omega
\displaystyle I = \int_\Omega f(x) \, \mathrm{d}\mu(x)
the domain: a band, a column, an image, a time interval or a probability space
\Omega, \quad \mu, \quad f : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}
on N nodes x_n, a rule with weights w_n
I \approx \hat{I} = \sum_{n=1}^{N} w_n \, f(x_n)
sumintegrix.rule
\hat{I} = \sum_n w_n \, f(x_n)
in log space \log \hat{I} = \mathrm{logsumexp}_n (\log w_n + \log f_n)

The running integral to every node, from either end: the optical depth down a column. A prefix sum of depth O(\log N), and its gradient is a reversed one.
