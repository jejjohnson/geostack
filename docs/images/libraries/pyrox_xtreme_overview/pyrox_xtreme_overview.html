pyrox-xtreme — three routes into the tail
Reduce a record to its extremes by block maxima, by peaks over a threshold or as events in time and space. Each route ends in a NumPyro likelihood, so the return levels come with a posterior.

a record x(t)
an xarray DataArray: a daily series, station records or a field on a grid
or events t_1 < \ldots < t_n
times (and places and marks) in a padded buffer with a mask
covariates
a warming index or a trend, for parameters that change, such as \mu(t)
a NumPyro model
priors on \mu, \sigma, \xi or on \lambda, and numpyro.sample(…, obs=…)
one tailits shape is \xi
{F(z) = \exp\{ -[1 + \xi (z - \mu) / \sigma]^{-1/\xi} \}}
the maxima of blocks follow the GEV above, smooth through \xi = 0
{y_k \sim \mathrm{GEV}(\mu, \sigma, \xi)}
excesses over a high threshold follow the GPD, with the same \xi
{\Pr(X - u > y \mid X > u) = (1 + \xi y / \sigma_u)^{-1/\xi}}
events in time or space follow an intensity
{\log L = \sum_i \log \lambda(t_i) - \int \lambda(t) \mathrm{d}t}
