pipekit-train — training as an operator
You bring a dataset, a model and a loss; one loop runs them on the backend you name and hands back the trained model as an operator, with a record of how it was made.
supplywhat to train
a dataset \mathcal{D}
a TrainingDataset of (input, target) pairs with a content_hash(): IterableDataset, SimulationDataset from a prior and a simulator h, or CachedDataset
a model g(\cdot\,; \boldsymbol{\theta})
an Equinox module in an EquinoxModelOp (or pipekit-jax's JaxModelOp), a Keras model in a KerasModelOp; for a Bayesian fit, a NumPyro model
a loss \ell or a task
MSE · NLL · KL · Composite, or a TrainTask, a KerasTask; a NumpyroTask for the Bayesian backends
the settings
optimizer_config, max_steps, batch_size, and callbacks such as EarlyStopping and Checkpoint
one loopTrainingLoop
{\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}} = \mathrm{argmin}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}} \ \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N} \ell\left(g(\mathbf{x}_n; \boldsymbol{\theta}), \mathbf{y}_n\right)}
the Bayesian backends give the posterior instead
{p(\boldsymbol{\theta} \mid \mathcal{D}) \propto p(\boldsymbol{\theta}) \prod_{n=1}^{N} p(\mathbf{y}_n \mid \mathbf{x}_n, \boldsymbol{\theta})}
every loop is a stateful operator
{\left(g(\cdot\,; \hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}),\ \text{artifact}\right) = \text{loop.run}()}
The Equinox backend takes max_steps optimiser steps, each on a batch \mathcal{D}^{(i)}; SVI steps on the whole dataset, and NUTS samples it in one call. HyperSweep runs one loop per point of a ParameterGrid and ranks the trials, on the validation metric when they have one.
backendsone extra each
Equinox with optax
"equinox"
backends.equinox — run · train_step · TrainState: adam, adamw, sgd or rmsprop, Grain batches, Orbax checkpoints, a ShardingSpec for a device mesh
NumPyro
"numpyro-svi" · "numpyro-mcmc"
backends.numpyro — run_svi, an AutoNormal guide and the ELBO by default; run_mcmc, NUTS
BlackJAX
"blackjax"
backends.blackjax — run: NUTS with window adaptation, on the model of a NumpyroTask or on a bare log-density
Keras 3
"keras"
backends.keras — run: Keras's own compile and fit on JAX, for a KerasModelOp or an Equinox model, which comes back in its own wrapper
outmodel and record
the trained model
an operator: the wrapper you passed in, or a NumpyroPredictiveOp that returns the posterior-predictive mean; it chains with | and sits in a Graph
the artifact
the loop's config, dataset_hash, trained_model_hash, the backend's final metrics, and uv pip freeze where uv is installed
checkpoints and logs
Orbax checkpoints on the Equinox backend, a JSONL line per logged step, and the model in a registry by its hash
pipekit-ops
The StatefulOperator every loop and sweep is, and the content hashes; its only required dependency.
[cycle] · [experiment]
pipekit-cycle's forward models and cycles; pipekit-experiment's TrainingArtifact as the record.
[cache] · [s3] · [gcs]
zarr, and fsspec with s3fs or gcsfs, for CachedDataset on disk, S3 or GCS.
