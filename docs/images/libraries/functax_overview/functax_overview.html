functax — one expansion, five operations
A field is a function, written as one basis expansion. Each module does one thing to it, and applying any plan, rule or transform is pure JAX, so jit, vmap and grad go through.
one expansiona field x over the coordinates \mathbf{s} \in \Omega
\displaystyle x(\mathbf{s}) \approx \sum_{d=1}^{D_z} z_d \, \phi_d(\mathbf{s}; \boldsymbol{\theta})
the coefficients, and the basis functions with their parameters
\mathbf{z} \in \mathbb{R}^{D_z}, \quad \phi_d(\,\cdot\,; \boldsymbol{\theta})
on N points \mathbf{s}_n, the basis matrix
\mathbf{x} = \boldsymbol{\Phi} \mathbf{z}, \quad \boldsymbol{\Phi} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times D_z}, \quad [\boldsymbol{\Phi}]_{nd} = \phi_d(\mathbf{s}_n)
interpolatefunctax.interp
read-out \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{H} \mathbf{x}
adjoint \mathbf{H}^\top \mathbf{r}
A plan reads x at the targets and sends the residuals \mathbf{r} back to the grid. Global schemes fit first: \mathbf{H}\, \mathcal{S}(\mathbf{x}).

on the sphere \nabla_S x, \quad \Delta_S x

integratefunctax.quad
\int_\Omega x(\mathbf{s}) \, d\mathbf{s} \approx \sum_n w_n \, x(\mathbf{s}_n)
A rule is data: the nodes \mathbf{s}_n and the weights w_n. integrix does the summing, scanning and adapting.

projectfunctax.project
\hat{\mathbf{z}} = (\boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \boldsymbol{\Phi} + \alpha \mathbf{I})^{-1} \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \mathbf{y}
minimises \lVert \boldsymbol{\Phi} \mathbf{z} - \mathbf{y} \rVert^2 + \alpha \lVert \mathbf{z} \rVert^2
From data to coefficients: ridge least squares, Galerkin with a rule's weights, or a variational fit with a roughness \lVert \mathbf{L} \mathbf{x} \rVert^2.
