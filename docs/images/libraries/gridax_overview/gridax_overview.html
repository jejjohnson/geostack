gridax: one C-grid, six operations
A staggered grid with one ghost ring and a mask on every point. Each operation reads or writes a piece of the finite-volume equation, and every function runs on NumPy, or on JAX arrays under jit, vmap and grad.
one C-grida tracer \bar{q} at T, the velocities u, v at U and V, arrays of shape (N_y, N_x) with the ghost ring
