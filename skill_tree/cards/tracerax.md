- **Provides.** Lagrangian particles in JAX: states and releases, velocities read from grids, deterministic, turbulent, diffusing and inertial steps under one loop, on the plane and on the sphere, deposits in cells, surface footprints, and the analysis of trajectories (FTLE, residence times, connectivity, dispersion, skill).
- **Start from.** `tracerax.particles.at_point`, `tracerax.velocity.GriddedVelocity`, `tracerax.advect.integrate`, `tracerax.advect.Langevin`, `tracerax.advect.RandomDisplacement`, `tracerax.advect.DeterministicSphere`, `tracerax.bin.Cells`, `tracerax.bin.Residence`, `tracerax.footprint.surface`, `tracerax.analysis.ftle`.
- **Keep.** A velocity is `velocity(t, position)`; positions are metres in a local frame, or (longitude, latitude) in degrees on the sphere; particles are masked and never dropped; a run has a start that may be traced and a static signed duration; the caller calls `jax.jit`, once.
- **Never.** Write a particle loop, a histogram inside a scan, a reflection at a wall or a step in longitude and latitude; write a `ParticleStep` or an `Accumulator` for the one loop, and pass the turbulence closure in.
- **Open the repository when.** You need more than these names, or a step it does not have: it has no skill yet, so read its `AGENTS.md` and its section of GeoStack's capability index.
