- **Provides.** What a sensor saw, located and converted pixel by pixel, on NumPy, JAX and other array libraries: Sun and view angles, air masses, parallax, calibration, reflectance, band averages, Planck's law, empirical retrievals, SAR calibration and altimeter corrections.
- **Start from.** `radiomax.geometry.solar_angles`, `radiomax.geometry.view_angles`, `radiomax.geometry.air_mass_factor`, `radiomax.geometry.earth_sun_distance`, `radiomax.radiometry.counts_to_radiance`, `radiomax.radiometry.reflectance`, `radiomax.radiometry.response_matrix`, `radiomax.thermal.brightness_temperature`, `radiomax.sar.dn_to_backscatter`.
- **Keep.** Degrees, `lon` before `lat`, and time as the UTC day and the second of the day; Planck's law is on wavenumber in cm⁻¹, with views per µm and per GHz; an invalid pixel is NaN with derivative 0.
- **Never.** Write an Earth–Sun distance, a geometric air mass, a Planck function or a Ross–Li kernel, or copy a coefficient table into a library; and never put a model of radiation through the atmosphere here, which is radtrax's.
- **Open the repository when.** You need more than these names: it has no skill yet, so read its `AGENTS.md` and its section of GeoStack's capability index.
